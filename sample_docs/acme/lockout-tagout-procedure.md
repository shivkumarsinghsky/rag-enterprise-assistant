# Lock-Out Tag-Out Procedure LOTO-07

Illustrative safety procedure written for the RAG reference implementation.

## Scope

LOTO-07 applies to all rotating equipment in the North Plant, including pumps, fans and compressors.

## Steps

1. Notify the control room operator and obtain a permit to work.
2. Stop the equipment and open the motor isolator.
3. Apply a personal lock and a danger tag to the isolator. Every technician working on the equipment applies
   their own lock.
4. Close and lock the suction and discharge valves for pumps.
5. Verify zero energy by attempting a start from the local panel.

## Removal

Locks are removed only by the person who applied them. A missing technician's lock can be removed only with the
written approval of the maintenance manager.
