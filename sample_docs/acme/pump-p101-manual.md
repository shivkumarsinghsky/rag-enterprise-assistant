# Boiler Feed Pump P-101 Maintenance Manual

Illustrative document written for the RAG reference implementation. Values are examples, not vendor data.

## Overview

P-101 is a multistage centrifugal boiler feed pump installed in the feedwater system of the North Plant boiler
house. It is criticality A: a failure stops steam production unless standby pump P-102 is started.

## Preventive Maintenance

The quarterly inspection of P-101 is performed every 90 days on a floating schedule using job plan JP-PUMP-90D.
The inspection covers coupling alignment, mechanical seal leakage, bearing temperature and vibration readings.
Bearings are lubricated every 2000 running hours with lithium-complex grease, 40 grams per bearing.

## Condition Monitoring Limits

Vibration on the drive-end bearing must stay below 4.5 mm/s RMS in normal operation. Between 4.5 and 7.1 mm/s
the pump is in the alert zone and an inspection must be scheduled within one week. Above 7.1 mm/s the pump must be
switched to standby pump P-102 and taken out of service.
Bearing temperature above 85 degrees Celsius is an alarm condition.

## Mechanical Seal Replacement

Seal kit part number SEAL-KIT-40 is required. Isolate the pump with lock-out tag-out (LOTO) procedure LOTO-07
before work starts. Replacing the seal takes approximately 4 hours for one mechanical technician.
