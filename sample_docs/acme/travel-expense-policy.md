# Travel and Expense Policy

Illustrative HR policy written for the RAG reference implementation.

## Hotels

Employees may book hotels up to 150 EUR per night in standard cities and 220 EUR per night in high-cost cities.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is 45 EUR when travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Approval

Trips longer than five days require approval from the employee's director before booking.
